Description:
Bethune Annex Café is a resident dining spot that accepts meal swipes and offers an all-you-can-eat experience. Celebrated for its innovative and eco-friendly design, the café boasts 11 live cooking stations, including an extensive salad station, healthy smoothies, global cuisines, fresh made pizza, and more. The Annex provides allergen-friendly options at its True Balance station.

The café frequently hosts events ranging from food holidays to special dinners. Students can check @huhospitality on Instagram to stay updated on events.

Accepted payment: Credit Card, Meal Swipes.

Sample stations that rotate through the week include: Ignite (Grill) — burgers, hot dogs, grilled chicken sandwiches, fries; Main Ingredient (Home) — rotating entrees like grilled chicken, glazed carrots, sweet potatoes; Crust (Pizza-Pasta) — cheese/pepperoni/specialty pizzas, baked mac; Savory Stack (Deli) — build-your-own sandwiches; Graze (Salad Bar) — full salad bar with multiple dressings; Tru Balance — allergen-conscious rotating dishes; Plant Forward — vegan/plant-based entrees; Sugar w/ Soul (Bakery) — cookies, cobbler, cupcakes; Oasis (Water Station) — flavored waters; a smoothie station.

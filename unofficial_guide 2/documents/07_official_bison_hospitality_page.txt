Welcome to Bison Hospitality:
Where exceptional dining meets the Howard University experience, our mission is to provide students, faculty, staff, and guests with fresh, flavorful, and nourishing meals that fuel body and mind. From bustling campus cafés and convenient markets to comfortable dining halls and grab-and-go options, Bison Hospitality offers something for every taste and schedule. We are committed to delivering high-quality service, a diverse menu, and a welcoming environment that reflects the vibrant Howard community.
